The humans behind Human Edge

Photo of Flick
Founder + Director

Felicity Barrow

Felicity Barrow is a leadership and culture specialist who believes the most powerful advantage leaders have, especially in an era of AI, is their humanity. A professionally trained actor with a psychology degree, a former senior leader, and an experienced organisational development consultant, Flick brings more than 20 years of lived leadership experience to her work. Her values are courage and kindness, and she is driven by a deep passion for human connection.

Flick understands the real guts leadership requires, not just commercially, but emotionally. She has seen how leaders can make people fly or cry, and she knows firsthand how hard leadership is, how high the human demand can be, and how rarely leaders are given the grace, support and vulnerability or safe space they need to learn and practice the human skills that matter most.

Across her career, Flick has watched the same challenges repeat in organisations: overloaded calendars, cultures that avoid feedback, leaders struggling with self-awareness, imposter syndrome, delegation, and the pressure to be “expert” at everything.

She also knows that while business has changed dramatically, human needs haven't, and the core human skills leaders need have only become more essential as work accelerates and technology becomes more pervasive.

Her greatest joy is witnessing those moments of self-awareness and empowerment in leaders; the realisation that “I can do this, and it's okay that I didn't know how.” Flick is committed to helping leaders build the human edge that transforms performance, culture and lives: keeping people accountable, enabling innovation, and creating conditions where humans can thrive.

She sticks to what she loves and what she's exceptional at: human relationships, authentic connection, honest conversations, courage, creativity, shared laughter and tears, and learning environments that feel alive.

This is why she founded Human Edge Leadership: to make leadership more human, more courageous and more impactful, one leader at a time.

“Crafted by a founder who blends creativity, acting, psychology, actual corporate leadership experience and real-world humanity … creating a unique experience.”
