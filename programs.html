Signature Leadership Experiences

the flagship

Our immersive leadership programs help leaders master the enduring critical human skills. This stuff directly impacts culture and performance.

Leadership Skills You Can't Skip

Many leaders rise on technical skill, but leading humans is a different craft entirely. Designed for those newer to leadership, or leaders thrown in without formal development, this program builds the human fundamentals every leader needs, no matter how new or seasoned they are.

Leaders learn to shape the habits that create trust, build connection, give meaningful feedback, foster psychological safety, develop their teams and lead inclusively, ensuring every person in a diverse workforce feels supported to reach their potential.

Through immersive, film-based storytelling and experiential practice, leaders explore the human realities behind performance and culture. They leave with practical tools, deeper self-awareness and the confidence to lead humans well.

What our programs cover (dependent on duration and modality):

Transition to leadership Imposter syndrome Building trust Psychological safety Listening Feedback Difficult conversations Managing conflict Leading for neurodiversity Coaching Leading through change Leadership habits Your leadership values

Presenters People Actually Want to Listen To

Fear of presenting never goes out of style, and neither do the confidently dull presenters draining our calendars. Scripted monotone speeches, an overwhelm of information and a lack of clarity make many presentations forgettable at best, and a frustrating waste of time at worst.

This program helps presenters find their natural voice and confidence with techniques we know work. With great habits, effective planning and learning to leverage those presenter's nerves to your advantage, you can be impactful, entertain your audience, make your point and feel empowered while you do it.

The ability to present yourself with confidence, think on the spot and communicate clearly are critical skills transferable to any setting. Our methodology is tried and tested.

The High-Impact Facilitator

Facilitation is wildly undervalued and rarely taught, which explains a lot about most meetings! Meetings are where culture lives and where innovation either ignites or dies.

This program transforms leaders into expert facilitators who confidently lead meetings with presence, manage group dynamics and create safe environments for challenge and constructive conflict, ensuring meetings save time rather than waste it.

Using film, storytelling and experiential practice, leaders experience what powerful facilitation feels like, and what it unlocks. They leave with the capability to run meetings that energise people, accelerate thinking and become the engine room of innovation.
